A — Street Brawler

Like Final Fight / Double Dragon. One ground plane, buildings are just scenery. Full focus on skating fast and fighting frat bros. Reach the frat door, kick it in, boss fight inside. Simple but very juicy to play.

Pros

  • Fastest to build
  • Combat is front and center
  • Mobile-friendly

Cons

  • Less "skateboard" feel
  • Flat — no grinding rails
ΑΘΣ
FRAT HOUSE
grind rails connect rooftops →

B — Rooftop Skater

Like Tony Hawk meets Donkey Kong. Street level + building rooftops are all platforms. Grind rails connect them. Fight on the street or up top. The frat house is the tallest building — you grind/jump your way up to the roof, then molotov it.

Pros

  • Most "skate game" feel
  • Vertical exploration
  • Grinding rails = satisfying

Cons

  • More complex to build
  • Harder on mobile
ΔΨΩ FRAT
street → curbs → awnings → frat balcony

C — Street + Frat Climb

Mostly street-level brawler, but with curbs, benches, fire escapes, and balconies as low platforms. The frat house has a climbable exterior — ledges, a balcony, then the roof. Best of both: accessible combat with a satisfying vertical finale.

Pros

  • Balanced complexity
  • Natural climb to boss
  • Good mobile feel

Cons

  • Needs careful level design
