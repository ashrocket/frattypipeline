A — Health Bar (Classic)

Simple health bar. Lose all health = life lost. A stylized "FRAT'D OUT" screen shows your character briefly transformed into a sorority girl before respawning. Clean, familiar, low friction.

Pros

  • Instantly readable
  • Mobile friendly
  • Easy to balance

Cons

  • Transformation is just a cutscene

B — Punk/Frat Tug-of-War

A single sliding meter. Punk on the left, frat on the right. Taking hits, touching frat items, or getting pinned by bros pushes it toward frat. Landing tricks and fighting back push it punk. Fill frat side 100% = life lost. Carry-over from the current game's DNA.

Pros

  • Matches existing game feel
  • Creates constant tension
  • Playing aggressively = defense

Cons

  • More complex to read quickly

C — Outfit Degradation

Your character visually transforms as you take frat damage — mohawk flattens, jean jacket becomes a polo, khakis appear, ponytail pops out. The transformation IS the health bar. Fully sorority-fied = life lost. Most expressive, most thematic.

Pros

  • Wildly thematic
  • No HUD needed for health
  • Visual storytelling

Cons

  • Hardest to implement
  • Subtle at small sizes
