A — Simple Brawler

Two attack buttons (A = skate attack, B = throw). Direction modifies the attack. Like River City Ransom — approachable, snappy, never feels complex. Good for mobile.

Pros

  • Instantly fun
  • Touch-friendly
  • Fast to implement

Cons

  • Less "Street Fighter" depth

B — Combo Fighter

Real Street Fighter DNA — light/heavy attacks, combo strings, a super meter. Directional special moves (like a skateboard DP). High skill ceiling. Hard on mobile without a fight pad.

Pros

  • Deep combat
  • Mastery curve
  • True SF feel

Cons

  • Brutal on touchscreen
  • Complex to implement

C — Skate-Combat Hybrid

Skating moves ARE combat moves. Ollie to stomp, kickflip to hit airborne bros, grind through enemies on a rail. A "trick meter" fills as you chain moves — full meter = a devastating finisher. Most unique feel.

Pros

  • Totally original
  • Skating + fighting unified
  • Trick meter = great flow

Cons

  • Needs careful tuning
  • Learning curve
